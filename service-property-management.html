Service Overview

Property Management

We support property owners with day-to-day management of residential and commercial properties, including tenant coordination, rent administration, maintenance and reporting.

Contact us to discuss your property and agree on the services and scope that suit your needs.

What's Included

  • Tenant sourcing and placement support
  • Rent collection coordination
  • Routine and emergency maintenance coordination
  • Property inspections and updates
  • Lease administration support
  • Utility and service charge coordination
  • Compliance and licensing coordination
  • Owner reporting

Our Process

Property Review

We discuss your property, goals and management requirements.

Management Proposal

We agree the scope, responsibilities, timeline and fees before work begins.

Ongoing Support

We coordinate agreed management services and keep you updated.
