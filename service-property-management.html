Property Management

Your property, professionally managed — maximised income, protected value, zero hassle.

Service Overview

Full-Service Portfolio Management

Owning property should be rewarding — not a second job. Sterling & Vance manages residential and commercial assets across Nairobi and beyond, combining rigorous tenant management with proactive maintenance and complete financial transparency.

Every property is assigned a dedicated portfolio manager, and every owner receives a detailed monthly statement covering income, expenses, occupancy and works.

What's Included

  • Tenant sourcing, screening & placement
  • Rent collection & arrears management
  • Routine & emergency maintenance coordination
  • Quarterly property inspections with reports
  • Lease administration & renewals
  • Utility, service charge & rates management
  • Legal compliance & licensing
  • Monthly owner statements & annual accounts

Our Process

Property Onboarding & Audit

We inspect your property, benchmark achievable rent, flag maintenance issues and prepare a management plan.

Marketing & Tenant Placement

Professional photography, portal listings and our tenant database — with full screening including references and checks.

Day-to-Day Management

Rent collection, maintenance, inspections and tenant relations — all handled by your dedicated portfolio manager.

Reporting & Optimisation

Monthly statements, annual performance reviews and recommendations to grow your yield and asset value.

Management Fees

Our standard fee is 8% of collected rent (minimums apply for small portfolios), with no hidden charges. A full fee schedule is provided in every proposal.
